Subsystem 5 full-chain runs on the car -- 2026-11-19
=====================================================

Three consecutive runs with Subsystem 3's line following live on main
(PR #17), a 750 ml bottle / ~100 mm obstacle on the line, bring-up speeds
cruise 5 cm/s and bypass 5 cm/s.

A temporary bring-up harness (NOT committed) stood in for the operator:
it waited past the boot tests, recalibrated line following in position,
armed it, abandoned any stale bypass and sent REMOTE_RESET / REMOTE_START.
There is still no console input path over USB, and the dashboard's
"send start" needs a WiFi build and an MQTT broker.

WHAT WORKS, verified on the car:
  - watch-ahead scanning: servo parked at 90 deg, sweeps only on a
    confirmed return ahead, parks again when the sweep finds nothing
  - detection at up to 247 mm, near the full OBS_DETECT_MM range
  - stop-and-rescan: the car HALTS, sweeps again while stationary, and
    obs_choose_side() picks the clear side from the fresh profile
    (chose LEFT on clrL=635 vs clrR=17; chose RIGHT on clrL=105 vs 635)
  - width 86..105 mm measured on a ~90..100 mm obstacle
  - the full four-turn bypass, every state in order, no aborts
  - reacquired=1: LEG_BACK cut short on EVF_NAV_LINE_FOUND

WHAT DOES NOT WORK YET:
  Line following does not HOLD the line after the handover.  It reports
  SUSPENDED -> REACQUIRING -> IDLE with CENTRE back on white (153..168
  raw).  Causes found and fixed in this commit, in the order they
  surfaced:
    1. the turn dwell did not scale with the bypass speed, so none of the
       four turns completed -- the path collapsed and the car returned to
       the obstacle
    2. OBS_LEG_BACK_MAX_MS was a flat LF_T_SEARCH_MS (2000 ms), only
       100 mm of travel at 5 cm/s against ~200 mm to come back
    3. the along-leg used the obstacle's WIDTH as a stand-in for its
       DEPTH, so the car turned back in before clearing it
    4. OBS_AV_TURN_STRAIGHT pivoted for a full turn dwell (2750 ms)
       BEFORE releasing, so the line was already behind the car when
       Line Following took over
  The last run in this file still predates fixes 3 (partly) and 4.

OPERATIONAL NOTE, the cause of several dead runs:
  The car must BOOT in Subsystem 3's start pose -- CENTRE on the middle of
  the line, RIGHT on white.  lf_cal_from_start_pose() refuses a
  low-contrast reading and then line following "never steers and reports
  TRACK_LOST once at run start".  A refused run looks identical to a
  hung car.  One failure recorded here: C avg 162 vs R avg 154, a
  contrast of 8 against the ~1000 of a good pose.

NFR14 IS RED IN THIS CONFIGURATION, deliberately.  At 5 cm/s a full
bypass runs to roughly 40 s against the 5.0 s limit.  See
OBS_BYPASS_SLOW_BRINGUP in config/timing.h for the one switch and two
speeds that restore it.
