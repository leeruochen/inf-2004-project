Subsystem 5 -- HC-SR04 effective beam width calibration
Date:   2026-11-04
Build:  DBG_BACKEND=USB APP_TEST=ON (dump_sweep() [S5RAW] output)
Target: rigid object 50 mm wide, held square to the sensor at ~158 mm.

PURPOSE
  obs_analyse() reported a 60 mm box as 190 mm wide (+280%), far outside
  NFR15 (width error within 10%).  OBS_BEAM_DEG was set to the HC-SR04
  datasheet's nominal 15 degrees.  These sweeps measure the real figure.

RAW SWEEPS (one line per bearing that returned an echo)

  [S5] obstacle 158 mm @ 80 deg w=145 clrL=635 clrR=635
  [S5RAW]   30 deg = 1039 mm      <- background (wall)
  [S5RAW]   40 deg = 1043 mm      <- background
  [S5RAW]   50 deg = 1068 mm      <- background
  [S5RAW]   60 deg =  175 mm      <- object
  [S5RAW]   70 deg =  170 mm      <- object
  [S5RAW]   80 deg =  166 mm      <- object
  [S5RAW]   90 deg =  165 mm      <- object
  [S5RAW]  100 deg =  158 mm      <- object
  [S5RAW]  110 deg =  170 mm      <- object
  [S5RAW] near=158 edges 60..110 deg width=145

  [S5] obstacle 158 mm @ 80 deg w=181 clrL=635 clrR=635
  [S5RAW]   30 deg = 1046 mm
  [S5RAW]   40 deg = 1043 mm
  [S5RAW]   50 deg =  191 mm
  [S5RAW]   60 deg =  170 mm
  [S5RAW]   70 deg =  158 mm
  [S5RAW]   80 deg =  158 mm
  [S5RAW]   90 deg =  158 mm
  [S5RAW]  100 deg =  170 mm
  [S5RAW]  110 deg =  174 mm
  [S5RAW] near=158 edges 50..110 deg width=181

ANALYSIS
  A 50 mm object at 158 mm subtends 2*atan(25/158) = 18 degrees.
  It returned an echo across 60..110 deg, a span of 60 degrees (and
  50..110, i.e. 70 degrees, on alternate sweeps).

  The HC-SR04 reports the nearest reflector ANYWHERE inside its cone, so
  the object keeps answering after the horn has turned away from it.  The
  excess span is therefore the beam:

      beam ~= observed span - true angular size
            = 60 - 18
            = 42 degrees        -> OBS_BEAM_DEG set to 40

  The datasheet's 15 degrees is roughly a third of the real figure, which
  is why the angular and lateral width estimates were both inflated.

CHANGES MADE (app_program/subsystem5/task_obs_scan.c)
  1. OBS_BEAM_DEG 15 -> 40, with the measurement recorded at the macro.
  2. The beam arc is now subtracted from the LATERAL EXTENT estimate as
     well, not only from the angular span.  The extent is smeared by the
     same beam, so before this it always won the max() and every object
     was reported about a beam-width too wide.
  3. OBS_WIDTH_MIN_MM (50) floor added.  With (1) and (2) a single-bearing
     run collapsed to 13 mm for the same 50 mm object, which is the unsafe
     direction: obs_leg_out_mm() would plan a turn too short to clear it.

RESULT (same object, same position)
  before:  190 mm   (+280%)
  after:    55 mm   (+10%)  on sweeps with a 60 deg span
            82 mm   (+64%)  on sweeps with a 70 deg span

  Mean error about +36%, and still biased WIDE, which is the safe
  direction per docs/DATA_REPRESENTATION.md 2.3.

OUTSTANDING
  NFR15 (+/-10%) is met on the better sweeps but not consistently.  The
  residual error is run-span jitter: one extra 10 deg slot at the edge of
  the run adds ~27 mm at this range.  Calibrating further needs objects of
  two or three known widths at two or three known distances; this fit uses
  a single object at a single range.
