INT-S5-01  Driving bypass on the car -- 2026-11-19
===================================================

Setup: Pico W on the mat, 750 ml Owala bottle ahead.  Serial capture at
115200, 290 s, covering the boot self-tests and the drive.

A temporary bring-up harness (not committed) stood in for Subsystem 3:
it sent REMOTE_RESET / REMOTE_START past the boot settle and then a
drep_nav_steer(0) every 50 ms, because task_lf does not publish on main
yet.  Nothing faked EVF_NAV_LINE_FOUND.

RESULT: PASS for detect -> avoid -> move away.
  Full four-turn bypass, every state in order, no aborts.
  Chose LEFT correctly (clrR = 17 mm, no room on the right).
  Range to the obstacle grew monotonically through the manoeuvre:
      detection 100 mm -> LEG_OUT 133 -> LEG_ALONG 169 -> LEG_BACK 215.

NOT VERIFIED: return to the track.  LEG_BACK expired with no line, as
expected -- nothing on main sets EVF_NAV_LINE_FOUND.  Control went back
to the VC, which re-commands forward at cruise, so the car then drove
blind into the bottle and Subsystem 4's IMU latched a lift fault
(cos_tilt = 830/1000, about 32 deg).  Every later bypass aborted with
"safety fault latched" until it re-armed.  Blocked on buddy3/lf3-link.

Two boot failures, both artifacts of the bottle standing in front of the
car during the 200 s settle, not regressions:
  S5-U05  echo line idle LOW      -- something was in range at that instant
  S5-U24  starts with no aborts   -- the avoid task had been cycling
                                     bypasses against the bottle
Re-run with a clear field at boot before recording either.

Fixes under test here:
  - straight legs re-send DREP_BYPASS_CONTINUE every OBS_LEG_REFRESH_MS,
    since Motion Control drops an unbounded command after
    MOTION_T_HOLD_MS (100 ms) and the legs are open-loop timed;
  - obs_avoid_abandon() returns the state machine to IDLE at run start.
    The previous run began in LEG_ALONG, inherited from the boot settle,
    and the first real detection could not open a fresh manoeuvre.
